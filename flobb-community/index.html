No. Membership and the certification course are completely free while we build the community. You only ever earn — you never pay to be part of Flobb.

No. We welcome both professional cleaners and cleaning enthusiasts. The Flobb Webinar Series takes you from the basics to commercial-grade methods.

We're building across Lagos first, with more Nigerian cities coming. Join the community now to get early access the moment we launch in your area.

Interested Webinar Attendees join the Flobb Cleaning Masterclass to access the Flobb Platform. Members get jobs weekly. Once you're certified and matched to client jobs, your earnings are held safely and paid out to your account every week — no chasing clients for cash.
