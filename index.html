How it works

From "I need a clean"
to "wow, properly cleaned".

Four steps. Sixty seconds to book. Same cleaner every time if you want her.

1

Pick a service

Multiple cleaning services, sized to your specific preferences. Choose a combination of our cleaning services. Toggle add-on services that suite your needs.

2

Match with a cleaner

Our support team reaches out immediately to confirm your order. Your job card is created and you are matched with a nearby vetted cleaner. Get your bill and make payment into the escrow account.

3

Support team keeps updated on the progress of work to make sure you are satisfied with the outcome.

4

Approve, then pay

Inspection report when they finish. Approve to release payment from escrow.

For Developers & Corporates

Cleaning beyond
expectations.

We specialise in two engagements where the cost of poor service is highest: post-construction deep cleaning — where handover condition reflects on a developer's reputation — and janitorial retainer services, where ongoing quality determines whether a facility holds its standard.

Document 01

Daily Productivity Tracking Sheet

  • Each operative's assigned units, start time & completion recorded daily
  • Quality pass/fail marked per unit by the supervisor — not self-reported
  • Daily summary: staff present, units completed, rework required
  • Supervisor-signed record issued every working day
Document 02

Unit Inspection Form

  • 20+ inspection points: General, Restroom/Kitchen, Detailing, Site
  • Pass/fail recorded per item before a unit is marked complete
  • Defects logged separately — protecting your snagging record
  • Dual sign-off: Team Lead and Supervisor both sign every form
Document 03

Site Supervisor Checklist

  • Site condition and access documented before work begins each day
  • Equipment, materials & PPE confirmed before the team starts
  • All seven cleaning categories verified complete per unit
  • Any incident or safety event recorded with corrective action

At every handover, clients receive a complete documentation package: per-unit inspection forms signed by both team lead and supervisor, daily productivity records for the full project, a before-and-after photo record, and a defect log that separates pre-existing issues from cleaning-related observations.

Got questions?

We'll help you out.

Is Flobb live yet?

We're building and testing in Lagos right now. You can already request a clean and we'll coordinate it directly over our web platform, and cleaners can join our community and start the certification. The full app follows — join now to get early access.

How does joining the community work?

Fill in the short form above. We'll open our web platform with your details so you can send them to us in one tap, then add you to the Flobb Cleaning Community. Webinar is free while we build, and members get the certification course, checklists, booking access and early platform access.

What does a clean cost?

Cleaning cost is dependent on the combination of services selected and the size of your space. The support team walks you through every detail before confirmation

How do I know the cleaner is trustworthy?

Every Flobb cleaner is ID-verified, reference-checked and interviewed in person before their first job, and carries ₦5m public liability cover. You approve the clean against before/after photos, and your payment is held in escrow until you're happy.

Do you handle offices and post-construction?

Yes. Alongside home cleaning we run janitorial retainers and post-construction deep cleans for developers and facilities, with full supervision and a signed documentation package at handover. Use "Speak to Jumoke" to start a project conversation.

Which areas of Lagos do you cover?

We're focused on Lagos to start — Lekki, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Yaba, Ikeja, Surulere and nearby. Not sure about your area? Send a request anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we can reach you.
